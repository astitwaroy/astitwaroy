### `~$ ls ~/projects --recent`

#### 🏥 ClinicQ: no more crowded clinic waiting rooms
Patients take a token from their phone and watch their place in line update live. The doctor's desk and the waiting-room TV stay in sync instantly, and wait times adjust to how fast the doctor is actually seeing people.

<a href="https://clinicq-84sn.onrender.com"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Try%20it%20live-39D353?style=for-the-badge&logo=render&logoColor=0d1117" /></a>
<a href="https://github.com/astitwaroy/clinicq"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" /></a>

`React` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Socket.IO` · no duplicate or skipped tokens even with 25 people booking at the same moment · 30 automated tests

#### 🎓 ScholarBuddy: find the scholarships you actually qualify for
Ask a question in plain language and get an answer backed by the official government guidelines, with the exact source quoted. An eligibility checker tells you clearly whether you meet each rule, don't, or need to check.

<a href="https://scholarsathi.onrender.com"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Try%20it%20live-39D353?style=for-the-badge&logo=render&logoColor=0d1117" /></a>
<a href="https://github.com/astitwaroy/ScholarBuddy"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" /></a>

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL + pgvector` `Gemini` `React` · picks the right scheme first in 97% of test questions · every quote is checked against the original document

#### 🤖 Visual Servoing Arm: a robot arm that follows what its camera sees
A simulated robot arm watches a moving target through a camera, spots it with computer vision, and smooths out noisy readings to track it, even live from a webcam.

<a href="https://github.com/astitwaroy/Visual-servoing-2D-arm-simulation"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-0d1117?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" /></a>

`Python` `OpenCV` `Kalman filter` · 52–73% less tracking error than using the raw camera readings

<sub>💡 The live demos run on free hosting and may take about a minute to wake up on the first visit.</sub>
